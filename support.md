  <h2>Common questions</h2>

  <h3>Does my script appear in the recorded video?</h3>
  <p>No. The script is drawn over the camera preview for you to read, and is never written into the file. Everything you export is clean footage.</p>

  <h3>How does Voice Follow work?</h3>
  <p>Instead of scrolling at a fixed speed, the script follows the words you actually say. Pause for breath and it waits; go off-script and it holds, then picks you back up when you return to the line. It uses on-device speech recognition — your voice is not sent to us or to Apple. It needs the Speech Recognition permission, which you can grant or revoke in Settings.</p>

  <h3>Can I use a remote or a foot pedal?</h3>
  <p>Yes. CueCaster responds to Bluetooth keyboards, presenter clickers and pedals that send key presses, so you can start a take, pause the scroll or change speed with the phone across the room on a tripod.</p>

  <h3>Can I bring in a script I already wrote?</h3>
  <p>Yes. Import from Files, iCloud Drive or another provider — plain text, RTF, Markdown, PDF and Word documents all come in.</p>

  <h3>Why does my export have a watermark?</h3>
  <p>Free exports carry a small watermark. CueCaster Pro removes it and exports at original quality.</p>

  <h3>How do I restore a purchase?</h3>
  <p>Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.</p>

  <h3>How do I cancel a subscription?</h3>
  <p>Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. The lifetime unlock is a one-time purchase with nothing to cancel.</p>

  <h3>Where are my recordings stored?</h3>
  <p>On your device, inside the app. You can play back, rename, share or save a take to Photos at any time. Deleting the app deletes them, so export anything you want to keep.</p>
